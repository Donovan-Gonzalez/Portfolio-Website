About Me

Hi! I’m Donovan, a senior Computer Science student at Macaulay Honors College at CUNY Queens College, graduating in May 2027. I’m currently a Data Cloud Intelligence intern on the Demo Pod team at Zeta Global, where I build client-facing demos, work with customer data, and explore AI tooling. Zeta is where MarTech hooked me. Seeing how a business turns raw data into real customer experiences changed how I think about technology: it’s not just about building systems, it’s about what those systems make possible for the business behind them. Today, almost every company’s success depends on how well its data infrastructure connects to the tools that act on it, and that’s the space I want to work in. That interest shapes my projects, from my Home Cloud Server, AWS Cloud Security & IAM Lab, and Cybersecurity Monitoring Lab, to my Macaulay capstone, a self-hosted Customer Data Platform. This site runs on that home server and serves as my portfolio. In my free time, I enjoy working out, playing video games, and exploring new tech. Feel free to reach out if you’d like to collaborate or talk tech!
